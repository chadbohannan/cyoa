# Follow-up: is there any free CYOA text? (2026-09-19)

Correction to the first research pass, which said "essentially none".

- Consider the Consequences! (Doris Webster & Mary Alden Hopkins, 1930) — the first known branching-narrative book. Public domain in the US: published 1928–1963 with no copyright renewal on record (intfiction.org thread 64048). Scan at https://archive.org/details/consider-the-consequences-1930/ ; a Twine port by Gregory Theriot (2023) at https://geetheriot.itch.io/consider-the-consequences and listed on IFDB. Three protagonists (Helen, Jed, Saunders), "a dozen or more" endings, original edition printed branch graphs.
- The Intercept (inkle) — small demo game written in ink, MIT-licensed with source at https://github.com/inkle/the-intercept. Real, complete, redistributable branching text.
- ChoiceScript's bundled sample (`web/mygame`) — under the ChoiceScript non-commercial license.
- ChooseYourStory.com — large community of user-authored "storygames", free to read, rights stay with authors/site. PocketDoc/Choose-Your-Story-Long-Text-Adventures on Hugging Face is scraped from it (marked not-for-all-audiences; license and whether branching is preserved not stated on the card).
- itch.io Twine/ink games — free to play; a minority are explicitly CC-licensed, but there is no filter for it.
- Twine cookbook and ink documentation examples — tiny, freely copyable snippets.
